More ways to name your files. Click NAME on any file to use the audio filename, the video filename, bump the version number or add a date — or set NAME ALL BY once for the whole batch. Your edited filenames now stay put when you change other settings. Also: a Save To folder option in the settings cog, a clearer warning when audio and video don't match in length, and your format and QC settings are remembered between sessions.
