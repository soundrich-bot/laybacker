Introducing the Stem Checker. Drag in a master and its stems and every file is drawn on one time axis, the master first, so you can see at a glance that each stem starts, stops and swells where it should. Play the audio and click between the stems to hear each one in situ, switching as it plays. A visual check by eye and by ear. If the filenames give the stems away, Laybacker offers the check as soon as you drop them; it is always there on the Audio Only page too.
