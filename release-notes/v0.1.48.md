Drop a video on its own and you get a small resizable player, a waveform for each channel, and what the audio measures. From there, you can make a working file (ProRes Proxy, LT, 422 or HQ, or H.264, and Laybacker remembers your choice), extract the audio as a WAV, or add a slate. The big button now says what it is about to do, so it only reads LAYBACK when a layback is happening. The drop zone keeps its shape while you drag, and the layback-only controls stay grey until there is a pair to lay back. Spelling follows your date format: English with the UK date, American with the US one. Every button now shares one typeface, size and case, the FFmpeg badge has gone, and a single sun button in the header cycles between different viewing modes.
