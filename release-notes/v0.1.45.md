QC now has a SPEC menu: EBU R128 (−23 LUFS ±1 LU, −1 dBTP) or the updated ATSC A/85 (−24 LKFS ±2 LU, −2 dBTP ±0.5 dB), and the tolerances QC judges with follow the preset rather than being fixed at ±1 LU. Type your own numbers and it becomes CUSTOM. One honest note in the help: Laybacker measures full-programme loudness, which is what R128 and A/85 short-form call for; A/85 long-form now asks for a dialogue-gated meter, which Laybacker doesn't provide.
