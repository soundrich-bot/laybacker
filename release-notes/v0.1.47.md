Slates get names, and the cards get tidier. In the slate editor, VIDEO NAME and AUDIO NAME drop the file's own name into the text — for a batch, every card gets its own. The preview frame slider now scrubs through the picture as you drag it, and the editor sits in two columns so the whole thing fits on screen; the app opens a little larger to suit. On the layback page each file now shows its details on a line of its own, with codec, frame size and frame rate for the video and sample rate, bit depth and channels for the audio, and QC results have moved to their own line so the buttons stay put. The filename field is now just called FILENAME.
